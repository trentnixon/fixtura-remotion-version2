## Your task

Design **fixture card components** — small match posters for upcoming games.

---

## Do NOT deliver

- Full page with 3 real fixtures assembled
- Results scorecard or ladder table
- Completed match stats

---

## DO deliver

- [ ] Fixture Card container
- [ ] Fixture Metadata Strip (+ long grade name)
- [ ] Home Team Block (+ missing logo)
- [ ] Away Team Block (+ missing logo)
- [ ] Fixture Centre — VS + date/time/ground (+ long ground two-line)
- [ ] Versus label treatment (centre column — **required** for Upcoming unlike Results)
- [ ] Compact Fixture Card (3-up density)
- [ ] Card **wireframe** — placeholder teams + centre meta

---

## Hierarchy

1. Team names + crests
2. Date / time / ground
3. Grade framing
4. VS (legible, narrow)

---

## Visual language

Reuse Team crest and Metadata strip from Results. Fixture cards may use a distinct container (poster vs scorecard) but must feel like the same Stadium Signal system.

**Note:** Upcoming **uses** Versus label; Results often omits it — do not copy Results team band blindly.
