# Fixtura — Greyscale Texture Background Prompt Library

A reusable prompt library for generating premium monochrome texture backgrounds for Fixtura automated sports graphics.

## Shared design principles

- Strictly monochrome: black, white and shades of grey only.
- Premium, editorial, modern sports-broadcast aesthetic.
- Texture and atmosphere over literal illustration.
- Preserve generous quiet areas for typography, scores, logos and automated content overlays.
- Avoid embedded text, numbers, team branding and logos.
- Avoid players and cricket equipment unless a future prompt explicitly calls for them.
- Avoid visual clutter and novelty effects.
- Target format: **1080 × 1350 px, 4:5 portrait**.
- Generate at high detail so assets can be reused, cropped and colour-mapped later.
