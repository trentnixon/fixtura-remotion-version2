# Fixtura Mascot Plate Concepts

A working catalogue of mascot-led background plates for Fixtura sports graphics. Each plate can express a club's mascot while leaving the team's actual crest or logo as a separate, accurate overlay.

## Plate system

Each mascot can support three complementary interpretations:

1. **Hero:** The creature, character or force is recognisable as the main visual.
2. **Trace:** Distinctive marks suggest the mascot without showing the whole figure: feathers, claws, tracks, scales or equipment.
3. **Atmosphere:** Movement, setting or energy associated with the mascot frames the content.

The hero suits a feature or single-match graphic. Trace and atmosphere can be quieter for dense results, ladders and player-performance assets. Positions below assume a portrait plate with scores and match information through the centre. Treat the listed character as a familiar visual association, not a rule every club must follow.

## First collection to prototype

**Eagle, Magpie, Seagull, Tiger, Lion, Bulldog, Bear, Shark, Dragon and Lightning.** This selection tests wings, fur, jaws, scales and abstract energy while covering a useful range of club identities.

## Production guidance

- Keep the central content zones legible. The mascot should frame or lead toward the information, not compete with it.
- Do not invent or imitate a club's official crest. Place the supplied crest separately from the generic mascot plate.
- For grayscale luminance masters, use Fixtura's defined colour-mapping bands and reserve the protected black and white cores for small intentional details. Check actual pixel distribution and recoloured output; an image prompt alone cannot guarantee the thresholds.
- Test each plate with dense and sparse content, dark and pale club palettes, and the final image or video export size.
- Confirm the mascot and its visual character with the club before using a plate in customer-facing content. A shared mascot name does not imply a shared illustration style.
