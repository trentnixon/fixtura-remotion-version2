## Agreed direction

The user agreed to this direction on 2026-09-13, including the delegated visual exploration.

Night Session brings the character of a premium televised cricket competition to club results: dark, disciplined, bold, and slightly industrial. Condensed typography, restrained club colour, quiet rules, and angular overlay details establish the identity. Full scores and team names remain the strongest elements within each match.

### Fixed requirements

- Reconsider proportions, alignment, and grouping while preserving required cricket structure, complete data, and conditional behaviour.
- Use a compact centred title lockup: a small supplied competition, round, or content label above condensed uppercase WEEKEND RESULTS. The club mark sits independently toward the upper-right as a broadcast identity stamp.
- Connect the mark and title region with restrained accent geometry. Use much less vertical space than the reference header. Exact dimensions and geometry remain proposed until frame review.
- Use one connected broadcast band for the complete team comparison. Do not divide the teams into separate cards. Mirror the groups with crests toward the outer edges and full scores toward the centre; each name sits directly beneath its own crest-and-score group.
- Centre the outcome beneath the comparison without a filled strip or pill. A short accent rule connects it to the band's lower boundary. The outcome sits against or slightly interrupts that boundary; the precise junction remains for schematic review.
- Attach Batting and Bowling beneath through alignment and shared thin rules, without additional boxes. Comparison, outcome, and performances form one vertical fixture unit.
- Use a quiet footer aligned with the match-content width, separated by one thin rule. Centre sponsor marks within the available region. Preserve Fixtura's existing sponsor sizing, ordering, and collapse behaviour. The footer concludes the broadcast frame without becoming another centred content lockup.
- Preserve distinct Batting and Bowling performance sections and their required ordering. Their geometry remains to be resolved.
- Follow existing responsive density rules. Match modules carry equal visual weight. Busy weeks tighten spacing and typography within safe limits.
- Missing performances collapse cleanly without enlarging the result statement or leaving awkward empty space.
- The page title is prominent but more space-efficient than the reference's LADDER heading.
- Intended reading hierarchy: Weekend Results title, grade or competition context, team names and full scores, result statement, player performances, then match metadata. The small label sits above the title but remains visually subordinate.
- Near-black and charcoal dominate overlay surfaces. The fixture primary colour is the main accent. Secondary colour appears sparingly for contrast, hierarchy, or small geometric details. Essential text is white or soft white.
- Overlays carry the identity independently of the background. Controlled opacity, dark surfaces, and contrast protection preserve readability across supplied backgrounds.
- Angular details stay restrained on overlay edges, section transitions, and occasional cropped perimeter details. They must not reduce usable content space.
- Translate useful reference relationships into Results, including the central title entry point and independent upper-right identity mark. Exclude ladder columns, ranking numbers, badges, and exact branded geometry.

### Creative direction

- Premium broadcast character with generous breathing room wherever the existing density rules allow it.
- Explore Teko for titles and scores, with Heebo for team names and supporting text.
- Narrow uppercase display lettering, a compact accent label where existing header fields allow it, and quieter supporting text.
- Thin, low-contrast rules and dark repeated surfaces provide rhythm without strong grids around every field.
- Compact, recognisable team crests act as sharp identity markers.
- Sponsor and organisation marks feel integrated into the composition. Avoid adding separate white boxes as decoration, while preserving existing placement and logo requirements.
- Uneven angular fragments and short parallel lines may form an original template signature within overlay surfaces.

### Open exploration

- Composition is being resolved through the interview. The designer may propose font selection, type scale, spacing, surface opacity, rules, and original angular geometry. Final refinement bounds remain pending. Preserve sourced anatomy and data behaviour, rather than unspecified starter geometry.
- Motion is not specified. This brief concerns static overlays. No animation implementation is authorised by the interview.
